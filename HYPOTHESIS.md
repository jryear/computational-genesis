# Computational Genesis — Lab 001

Protocol version 1. Frozen before participant development. Historical `src.lab`
pilots are exploratory and do not count toward these claims.

## H1 — Functional generalization and recovery

An independently developed inventor implementation can satisfy every specified
response on six unseen synthetic cases, including mixed domains and interrupted
delivery, in three fresh repetitions, within the fixed limits. One wrong response,
lost acknowledged mutation, duplicate effect, invalid checkpoint, timeout or resource
failure falsifies H1 for this submitted implementation and protocol.

## H2 — Joint execution improvement

Conditional on both frozen implementations passing H1's complete correctness and
recovery gate, the inventor will reduce median whole-suite workload CPU by at least
20% AND total checkpoint bytes by at least 25% relative to the conventional baseline.
It must not increase median active request latency by more than 10%. Otherwise H2
is falsified for this pair. If the correctness gate fails, H2 is ineligible, not a win.
Three paired repetitions cannot establish statistical superiority or population effects.

## H3 — Architectural novelty is not assumed

Record the mechanisms actually implemented: state organization, dependency handling,
deduplication, checkpoint format and recovery. A familiar map, event log, task graph,
reducer or actor remains familiar even if renamed. Architectural novelty is descriptive,
not a success gate, and is not inferred from model prose.

## Interpretation

Results concern two bounded design attempts on one Python/single-host substrate.
Failure does not refute all alternative architectures; success does not establish
a new operating system, distributed consensus, or production security. No GUI,
kernel, emulator, scheduler framework or driver is required.

The independent evaluator's negative controls must fail and positive control must
pass before participant results are admissible. Neither participant may change
this document, the substrate contract, grader or hidden corpus. All submissions,
development feedback, errors and final trials are retained. Integration beyond
this lab requires separate explicit approval.
